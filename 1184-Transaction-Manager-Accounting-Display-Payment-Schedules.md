##### Display Payment Schedules

App ID: TJ04

With this app, you can display a comprehensive list of all treasury-related payments that have been made or that are still due. The list also contains information about the business partners and bank details.

**Key Features**

This app provides the following key features:

Display a list of payments across the Treasury component

Define the layout of the payment schedule by choosing and arranging report columns

Save your selection criteria as a variant

Export the list of payments to a spreadsheet

**Procedure**

- 1. Open the Display Payment Schedules app on the SAP Fiori launchpad.
- 2. The Treasury: Payment Schedule screen appears. Enter your selection criteria in the corresponding fields.
- 3. Choose Execute. A list appears displaying payments with business partner information and the bank details used for processing.
