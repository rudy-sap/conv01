Here you get more details about terms used in risk management.

|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|Financial Object|In Treasury and Risk Management, financial data is analyzed based on the financial objects created automatically for each object you want to analyze. Additional information that is required for risk analysis is also entered into the financial object. For more information, see also Financial Objects. |
|Analysis Structure|The analysis structure is the organizational unit used to analyze and control risks in businesses. The analysis structure is the highest-level organizational and evaluation unit in risk analysis. The analysis structure contains the analysis characteristics. These characteristics are your evaluation criteria. Your portfolio hierarchies are based on the characteristics belonging to the analysis structure. For more information, see also Analysis Structure. |
|Portfolio Hierarchy|The portfolio hierarchy is a hierarchical structure for organizing characteristics, so it influences how the results are displayed in an evaluation. The characteristics must belong to the assigned view (if portfolio hierarchies are used to arrange the characteristics of a view into a structure). For more information, see also Portfolio Hierarchies. |
|Base Portfolio|A base portfolio is the smallest valuation unit in structured reports for risk analysis. A base portfolio is determined for every combination of characteristic values. For more information, see also Base Portfolios. |
|Risk Hierarchy|Hierarchical grouping of risk factors for value-at-risk evaluations. It is used to calculate risk values on individual consoldiation levels, and aggregate them into a value for overall risk. Market data changes are calculated only those risk factors contained in the risk hierarchy. For more information, see also Risk Hierarchy. |
|Maturity Band|A maturity band defines the sequence of time periods and governs in which time periods the results of evaluations are shown. For more information, see also Maturity Band. |
