### Treasury Position Flows - Read

> **Path:** APIs for Treasury and Risk Management > Treasury Position Flows - Read | L2 | trm12 p.2 | loio `fdbdf48471784561a68d2a23d474650d` | [portal](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/d9c0dd5023e34f0d85ed30fc859496aa/fdbdf48471784561a68d2a23d474650d.html?locale=en-US)

Technical name: API_TRSYPOSFLOW_SRV

This synchronous inbound service enables you to read treasury position flows. It is based on the OData protocol and can be consumed in Fiori apps and on other user interfaces.

The treasury position is the most detailed level in which you can break down the balance sheet accounts in the Treasury and Risk Management ledger. On the Treasury positions level, the key date valuations are made and the position components are managed. The treasury position flows are generated by the operative or derived business transactions to update the position components of the treasury positions. The service provides you with detailed information about each selected treasury position flow, such as amount, currency, dates, differentiation criteria of the affected treasury position, business transaction, and update type.

This service is published on the SAP Business Accelerator Hub. For more information about APIs, see APIs on SAP Business Accelerator Hub.
